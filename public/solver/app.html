Hot Seat Poker · Solver app

Do you need it?

Probably not for most spots. Turn and river spots, and flops with the Quick or Standard bet sizes, solve right in the browser with nothing to install.

Browsers can only use about 4 GB of memory, so bigger trees (several bet sizes on the flop, or very wide ranges) need this free app. It runs the solve on your computer with all of its memory and CPU, and everyone in your lobby sees the results.

Download

Download Windows Linux

Windows

  1. Open the downloaded zip file and drag hot-seat-solver.exe to your desktop (or any folder).
  2. Double-click it. Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” because the app isn't from a big company: click More info, then Run anyway. You only do this once.
  3. A black window opens saying Hot Seat Solver is running. Leave it open while you study.

Mac

A Mac version isn't available yet. On a Mac, use the Quick or Standard bet sizes, which solve right in the browser.

Linux

  1. Unzip, then run ./hot-seat-solver in a terminal and leave it running.

Use it

  1. On the solver (at the table or on the solver page), click Connect the solver app under the Solve button.
  2. Your browser may ask whether hotseatpoker.com can access devices on your network. Choose Allow: that's how the page talks to the app on your own computer.
  3. From then on, solves run in the app. This browser remembers it and reconnects by itself next time, as long as the app is open.

Good to know

  • Nothing is uploaded: the page sends the spot to the app on your computer, and only Hot Seat Poker pages can use it.
  • A big flop can need 4 to 16 GB of memory and a few minutes. The app says how much before it starts and won't start a solve your computer can't hold.
  • In a lobby, students see your solve's results through the lobby; they don't need the app. Keep the app and your page open until you're done reviewing.
  • The app is open source (GNU AGPL v3), built from the code on GitHub, using postflop-solver by Wataru Inariba.
