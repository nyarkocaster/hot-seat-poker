Deal the hand you want to teach

The teacher picks every hole card and every board card, or leaves any slot to the deck. Set the stacks and blinds, deal, and act for any seat. Undo a mistake, cancel a hand, or replay the same cards so students can try another line.

Students play from their own seat

Students open the site on a phone or laptop, pick your lobby, and tap an open seat. No accounts. Each student sees only their own cards until you press Reveal all cards, which also shows the hands that folded.

Teach together

Co-teachers join with the lobby's teacher code. You see each other's pointers on the table, and an outline marks whatever another teacher is editing, so two people don't change the same seat at once.

Solve the spot you just played

Once a hand reaches the flop with two players, load it into the solver beside the table. Everyone sees the same answer and can move through it together. Bets and cards at the table move the solver along with the hand.

Free to use

Nothing to install for the table, and lobbies are free. Solves run on the computers in your lobby, not on a paid server. The code is open source.

Start a lobby, give your students its name, and deal your first hand. Want to try it alone first? The practice table runs on your device with no lobby.
